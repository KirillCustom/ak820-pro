Answers to the four questions:

1. **Input gate: yes.** A processed press, or decoded encoder event, is sufficient for this gate; 50 ms provides a bounded no-input outcome. Delaying the five-second battery poll is harmless. Keep RX parsing, ACK handling and report transmission running. This does not establish exact host delivery or protect subsequent taps from V2.

2. **Budget: no—must-fix.** A 20 ms timeout can still share its pass with three synchronous battery glyphs, approximately **7.5 ms**, or a debug-page clear. Earlier input processing can itself spend **8 ms** on modified-consumer ordering. Measure the budget from the whole pass’s start, reserve the transaction’s worst-case cost, and defer competing display/animation work. See [display.c:1804](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/graphics/display.c:1804), [display.c:788](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/graphics/display.c:788), [consumer_mod.c:51](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/consumer_mod.c:51). Also cover the synchronous [legacy RTC SET](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/rtc/rtc.c:1105) and [two-transaction STOP recovery](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/rtc/rtc.c:1429) under the same arbitration.

3. **Log: no—must-fix.** The locked copy isolates accumulators, but resumed `log_task()` can reach a boundary, publish an entry and reset live data **before step 4**. “Pending” only hides reads. Explicitly prohibit ordinary closure/reset until reconciliation; continue collecting live observations. Define merging when sleep crosses **zero** boundaries, and correct allocation when the post-wake remainder crosses a boundary during recovery. Otherwise entries can be reordered or observations lost. The relevant writer/reset is [battery.c:1357](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/battery.c:1357), [1382](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/battery.c:1382).

4. **Repaint terminal cases: sufficient to terminate.** **Should-fix:** step 4 finishing does not mean the battery row has repainted, and step 3 can supersede step 2’s clock. Track completion against the latest clock correction and post-debit battery paint. The 250 ms release must explicitly permit stale/incomplete pixels on failure; it cannot coexist with an unconditional “never shows stale time/level” promise. Battery painting remains incremental at [display.c:1809](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/graphics/display.c:1809).

**Additional must-fix — preserve the acquired second across split reads.** [§4 step 3](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/plans/DEEP-SLEEP-BUILD-PLAN.md:284) introduces inter-pass delays. If the full read occurs one second after the bracket, the existing confirmation only compares that full read with the final read; the old edge timestamp plus the `999 ms` clamp can set the clock nearly a second wrong. Require the full calendar to match the bracketed second and reject/reacquire an expired bracket. Check timeout/cancellation even while bus admission defers work. See [rtc.c:1474](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/rtc/rtc.c:1474), [1481](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/rtc/rtc.c:1481).
