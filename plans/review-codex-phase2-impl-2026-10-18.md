1. **Must-fix — The effect “restart” does not repaint Jellybean Raindrops.**

   `apply()` calls `rgb_matrix_mode_noeeprom(current_mode)`, but this QMK implementation merely sets `rgb_task_state = STARTING`. The next render sets `params->init` only when the **effect or enable state changed**. Neither changed here. Jellybean consequently continues updating individual randomly selected LEDs; existing pixels retain their previous brightness through dim, off, cut, and restoration.

   This leaves the original protection defect unresolved. Add a mechanism that actually refreshes every affected pixel, and make darkness independent of an effect’s update pattern. [power.c:102](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/power.c:102), [rgb_matrix.c:605](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/quantum/rgb_matrix/rgb_matrix.c:605), [rgb_matrix.c:346](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/quantum/rgb_matrix/rgb_matrix.c:346), [jellybean_raindrops_anim.h:22](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/quantum/rgb_matrix/animations/jellybean_raindrops_anim.h:22).

2. **Must-fix — The upper band still mishandles uncertainty crossing the top knot.**

   The transformed endpoints and signed subtraction are correct within the measured curve. But `band_upper()` still returns `900 + 15 = 915` whenever `V + 25 >= 4036`. It only avoids this ceiling when the **reported estimate itself** qualifies as clamped.

   For example, `5C = 98` gives 4019 mV and an upper edge of 91.5%, although its allowance extends to 4044 mV, outside the measured curve. A valid higher count is therefore ratcheted down. This is precisely the boundary case identified in the original review; widening the upward allowance expands the affected region to **4011 mV and above**.

   Treat an upper endpoint reaching the unmeasured region as unbounded up to 100%, consistently in normal counting and re-seat/handover bounding. 5. **Must-fix — Handover bounding lasts only one report.**

   `adopt_relaxed()` correctly bounds a known level, then clears `adopt_owed`. On the next idle-USB report, `level_report()` resumes walking directly toward `curve(V)`.

   For example, a known 70% inside the relaxed band can survive adoption, then climb toward an 85% curve estimate without further charging. That reinstates the plateau error the band was introduced to avoid and contaminates the next charging/discharging start.

   Preserve the bounded estimate after handover, or explicitly redesign that idle-USB policy. The current test accepts a broad range between the lower edge and the curve, so it does not establish that the bounded result persists. 6. **Must-fix before accepting the validation gate — The replay is scored only at entry endpoints.**

   `rp_play()` advances firmware throughout each interval, but `c_replay()` checks only `r->r_level` after the entire entry. It never installs a per-second accuracy hook. Thus the reported extrema are **ten-minute endpoint extrema**, not extrema at every simulated second. Drain 2e stops before its reboot; it does not exercise that reset or the remaining drain. [count.c:248](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/scripts/battery_sim/count.c:248), [sim.c:975](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/scripts/battery_sim/sim.c:975), [run.sh:65](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/scripts/battery_sim/run.sh:65).

   The report reconstruction also materially affects the band. It turns each entry’s extrema into a monotone ramp, choosing direction from adjacent means and spacing reports evenly. On a slowly changing plateau, extrema can represent noise; sorting them creates sustained excursions that survive trimming and operate the ratchet.

   Reproducing that reconstruction for R3’s **10:20 entry on October 16** gives a final trimmed estimate of about **94.62**, versus the logged period mean **95.84**—an additional **10.6 mV downward displacement caused by the reconstruction**. This does not invalidate the replay, but it prevents attributing all the required band widening to physical cycle variation. [sim.c:884](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/scripts/battery_sim/sim.c:884), [sim.c:965](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/scripts/battery_sim/sim.c:965).

   Score every second, and compare several admissible report orderings: interleaved dithering, correlated bursts, and sustained excursions. Keep the current ramp as one stress case.
