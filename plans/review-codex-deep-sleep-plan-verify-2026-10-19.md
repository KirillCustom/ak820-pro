Remaining findings:

- **Must-fix — “first pass” cannot guarantee the waking report.** §4 step 1 assumes scanning also completes debounce. This board uses deferred per-key debounce: the first changed sample starts a **5 ms** counter; a later scan transfers the press. The shared matrix may also initially contain pre-wake samples. A subsequent 20 ms PCF read can therefore consume a short tap before it becomes a report. Make step 1 wait across passes for actual input processing, with a bounded no-input outcome. Also inhibit the **existing periodic battery poll**, not just the new step-5 poll, until that gate clears. See [debounce:37](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/quantum/debounce/sym_defer_pk.c:37), [matrix consumption:1043](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/drivers/led/sn32f2xx.c:1043), [automatic poll:1159](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/bluetooth/ch582f_ajazz.c:1159).

- **Must-fix — #34 needs scheduling, not only validation.** The acquisition edge still performs three transactions in one call. Each has its own timeout, and ordinary display/10 Hz/persistence work can run afterward. `pcf_read()` also invokes the synchronous LCD bus guard. Specify a shared pass budget, split the acquisition edge, defer competing expensive work, and admit PCF transactions only with the bus idle. Include the pre-sleep read. See [acquisition:1471](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/rtc/rtc.c:1471), [bus guard:224](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/rtc/rtc.c:224).

- **Must-fix — the log is not frozen by pausing `battery_task()`.** `ch582_task()` directly calls `battery_5c_report()`, which changes `lg_c5_*` during recovery. Normal polling remains active. Take an actual entry snapshot and separate or defer post-wake intake. Flush pending pre-entry awake energy too: `energy_tick()` normally runs only once per second. Two related corrections: the first mixed entry’s ending level must include its sleep debit, rather than remain “as at entry”; and `HC_BATTLOG` needs a pending/retry response until sleep time and entries commit, otherwise an early dump dates old entries hours too recently. See [report callback:1343](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/bluetooth/ch582f_ajazz.c:1343), [battery cadence:1480](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/battery.c:1480).

- **Must-fix — page 1 exceeds 29 bytes.** §8 now includes a separate sequence number. Even with a **u8** wake source, five u32 fields + three u16 fields + four identification/status bytes = **30 bytes**; interpreting wake source as another u32 gives **33**. One 26-byte layout is: four u8 fields `{0x81, wake, validity, recovery}`, four u32 fields `{sleep_count/sequence, total_s, last_PCF_s, counted_s}`, then three u16 fields `{phase_ms, PLL_loops, strays}`. Sharing count/sequence requires explicitly defining them as the same counter; otherwise remove a field or add a page.

- **Must-fix — the repaint hold needs terminal cases.** With `DISPLAY_CLOCK_OFF`, no clock face is painted; Fn+D can also own the panel after wake. A literal “wait for clock paint” can leave the backlight dark and recovery held indefinitely. Define completion for clock-off, unchanged date, alternate panel ownership and failed repaint. Release only after the required glyphs **and final DMA** complete: queue-empty alone is insufficient. See [clock-off:1224](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/graphics/display.c:1224), [panel ownership:2954](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/graphics/display.c:2954), [queue reset after DMA arm:2704](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/graphics/display.c:2704).

- **Should-fix — count supply samples after battery processing resumes.** §4 step 7 cannot obtain “1.5 s of fresh samples since wake” while step 0 pauses their producer. Require actual post-resume samples and a bounded failure outcome. See [supply sampling:1456](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/battery.c:1456).

- **Should-fix — Minimal’s wake message has a circular gate.** Requiring a lit screen before posting prevents the message that is supposed to light Minimal’s screen. Permit this notice to paint while dark, then apply its four-second illumination after repaint recovery. See [power.c:209](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/keyboards/a_jazz/ak820pro/power.c:209).

**Timer and other checks:** SN32 manual p.125 explicitly makes CEN control **both TC and PC**; CRST is the separate reset. Clearing CEN preserves their state. No prescaler reset, counter rewrite or VT-list adjustment is needed. Restore CEN on every exit, including no-sleep unwinds, before unlocking. The pended SysTick handler computes actual elapsed counter ticks and re-arms the next deadline; QMK’s half-wrap maintenance VT remains valid. See [manual](/Users/jdlien/code/ajazz-ak820-pro/docs/SN32F299_V1.8_EN.pdf), [VT processing:508](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/lib/chibios/os/rt/src/chvt.c:508), [QMK maintenance:36](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/platforms/chibios/timer.c:36).
