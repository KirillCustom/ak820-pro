What the change does:
1. Dev builds only: an inbound 5A trace in ch582f_ajazz.c. Every checksum-valid 5A frame is recorded (raw byte, ms since the previous frame) in a 56-slot ring, with counts of frames and of accepted ones (the ones stored in host_leds), the last byte, and the age of the last frame. HC_DEVHOOK ops 7 (header), 8 (ring page 0..3, 14 {byte, gap} pairs), 9 (reset) read it over raw HID (hid_protocol.c). Purpose: a hands-free test of which LED bits the module forwards (the BLE descriptor declares 5 bits: num, caps, scroll, compose, kana), whether a repeated value is forwarded, and how close together frames arrive (the Mac completes one acknowledged write per ~30 ms).
2. All builds: the LCD lock band's Scroll Lock indicator is shown "settled". lock_state_scroll() now returns scr_shown, which follows the host's Scroll Lock only after it has held 1500 ms (checked in update_leds() at 10 Hz), except that a change within 500 ms of this board's own KC_SCRL press shows at once (led_update_kb). Motivation: the channel toggles Scroll Lock up to ~33 times a second and restores it at the end of each frame; shown as sent, the band repaints each 10 Hz tick, and one main-loop pass >= 25 ms (marked "blit") happened during a probe burst on 10-17. The 25 ms main-loop budget is the project's hard line: below it a keypress cannot be lost.
