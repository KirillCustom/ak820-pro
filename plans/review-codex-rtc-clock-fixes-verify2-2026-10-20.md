**Note — Holding STOP during the slew deliberately blocks deep sleep.** `rtc_sleep_ok()` rejects the active slew, active writer, and pending request. The waiting step returns immediately, so it does not block the main loop. After settling, `ps_plan()` replaces the old calendar and deadline; a subsequently missed release window still returns through `PS_STOP_WRITE` and plans again. The long STOP interval does not itself break that restart path.
