Why: flash 2h was measured on the board (plans/LOCK-LED-CHANNEL-PLAN.md, "Measured on the board, flash 2h"): the module passes all eight bits of the LED byte, including the padding bits 5-7 (the board's existing `d <= 0x1F` guard keeps those bytes out of QMK's LED state, but the receiver is fed the raw byte); 17 of 18 frames committed, the miss one 366 ms write mid-frame past the 150 ms gap. So 2i:
1. adds profile P3: bits 3-7 (0x08-0x80), base 31, 29 bits in 6 digits; its own preamble (bits 5-7 flipped together three times, from bits 5-7 clear); its header in P3; a P3 preamble may announce only P3; the trailer as P1's. Num, Caps and Scroll never move under P3.
2. raises LOCKCHAN_GAP_MS from 150 to 500 ms (between transitions once synced).
3. changes the preamble's spacing rule. With the gap at 500, the host tests found a stray Num+Scroll change 300 ms before a P0 frame passing for the preamble's first transition (the receiver synced one write early and lost the frame). Now LOCKCHAN_QUIET_MS is 150 (was 100) and is also the preamble's limit: a transition at least QUIET after the last one starts a preamble, a sooner one continues it. poll() gives up a preamble at QUIET.
4. a sender rule, in the spec: the warm-up comes more than 500 ms after the sender's last write, so a receiver left mid-section by a lost or abandoned frame has given it up (without it the host tests lost 703 of 30,000 clean frames following a faulted one: the stuck section ate the warm-up and the preamble as digits).

The spec is plans/LOCK-LED-CHANNEL-PLAN.md, section "The wire format" (updated for 2i).
