3. **Must-fix — define fix 3 as an interrupt-service-gap monitor, and report context rather than cause.**

   Consecutive entry timestamps measure an **inter-entry gap**. Even without missed ticks, subtracting 50 µs yields the difference between successive service delays, not the current delay. With missed/coalesced matches, it also includes skipped periods. The GPT handler consumes a latched match flag; it does not replay every elapsed tick. [GPT LLD:565](/Users/jdlien/code/ak820-pro/qmk_firmware-ak820pro/lib/chibios-contrib/os/hal/ports/SN32/LLD/SN32F2xx/CT/hal_gpt_lld.c:565)

   At priority 2, the monitor can be delayed by **UART2 successfully servicing traffic** and by USB that priority-0 UART2 can preempt. Those are false positives for UART starvation. 6. **Should-fix — budget the monitor and handle discontinuities explicitly.**

   At 20 kHz, **each added microsecond costs 2% of the entire CPU**. Counter updates, context capture, function-call overhead and exception-return work exceed “a counter read and a compare.” Use the raw CT16B5 counter, constant integer thresholds, and a small rare-event path; measure scan rate and complete-pass timing with the monitor off/on.
